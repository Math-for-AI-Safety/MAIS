# Provenance — MAIS-P3

Gautam Neelakantan Memana submitted the dead-neuron counterexample to [MAIS issue #1](https://github.com/Math-for-AI-Safety/MAIS/issues/1), resolving [MAIS-O60](../../open-problems/MAIS-O60.md) in the negative. GPT-5.6 Sol prepared the strengthening published as an appendix, and Claude Fable 5 independently checked the result for MAIS. Lionel Levine invited the revised paper as MAIS-P3.

The author maintains the manuscript on [arXiv:2608.04451](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04451). This repository carries its MAIS landing record rather than a separate copy of the manuscript.
