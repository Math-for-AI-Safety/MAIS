# Contributing discussion or progress on a MAIS Open Problem

Not every contribution is a solution. Ideas, partial results, approaches that failed (negative information is information), numerical experiments, reformulations, connections to literature the problem page missed — all of it is welcome.

1. **Post to the problem's issue thread** — one issue per problem; if none exists yet, [open one](https://github.com/Math-for-AI-Safety/MAIS/issues/new/choose) titled with the problem's identifier ("MAIS-On: …"). Attach notes or link a draft as convenient. Polish is not required: the [Polymath rule](../CONTRIBUTING.md#rough-ideas-are-welcome) applies — share rough ideas rather than waiting.
2. **A light check, not a review.** Discussion posts get a single screening for three things: good faith, legibility (could a mathematician follow what you're claiming?), and bearing on the problem as stated. There is no adversarial verification unless you claim a solution — if you do, the [solutions path](solutions.md) applies.
3. **The record.** Substantial progress gets linked from the problem's page — or collected in the problem's folder, once it has one — credited to you. If the discussion grows into a solution, step onto the [solutions path](solutions.md) from wherever you are.
