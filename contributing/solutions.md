# Submitting a solution to a MAIS Open Problem

So you think you've solved one of the [open problems](../open-problems/README.md)! (For progress short of a solution, there's a [lighter path](discussion.md).)

1. **Say so early**, ideally before you invest serious work: [open an issue](https://github.com/Math-for-AI-Safety/MAIS/issues/new/choose) titled with the problem's identifier ("MAIS-On: …"). This protects you from duplicating effort, lets others see the problem is being worked on, and puts the credit question on the table from the start.
2. **Post the solution to the same issue** — attach a PDF or link a preprint. One issue per problem. Partial and experimental results are welcome; label their status (e.g. "numerical, not yet proved").
3. **Verification.** Expect an adversarial review. Questions, gaps, and repairs are worked out in the issue thread, in public. Note: This automated review is designed to surface issues with a draft before submitting it for publication; it does not substitute for the human researcher judgment supplied by peer review.
4. **The record.** Once verified, the problem's page records the resolution or progress, credited as printed on your write-up. The work stays yours: MAIS [cites contributed work as its authors' own](../CITATION.md), and AI coauthors are welcome. You're encouraged to post to arXiv, and invited to submit a polished version as a MAIS paper when it warrants one.

**Was it already solved?** "Open" means open to our knowledge. If you find an existing solution in the literature, please [open an issue](https://github.com/Math-for-AI-Safety/MAIS/issues/new/choose) to tell us about it!  Finding an existing solution [counts as resolving the problem](../CONTRIBUTING.md#substantial-contributions), and you'll be credited with finding it on the problem page.
