# Proposing a new problem or agenda

MAIS collects problems whose solution would advance AI safety and which are stated precisely enough for a mathematician outside the area to attack. If you have one, it is welcome here under your name.

First check the [registry](../open-problems/README.md): a restatement of an existing problem keeps its identifier rather than earning a new one.

Then [open an issue](https://github.com/Math-for-AI-Safety/MAIS/issues/new/choose) with:

- the **statement**, as precise as you can make it;
- the **motivation** — the AI safety question it serves, stated so that a reader with no MAIS context can follow it;
- **what's known** — the nearest results, and why existing tools fall short;
- a **difficulty guess** — ★ starter · ★★ research project · ★★★ likely needs a new idea.

If it lands, the problem receives the next free O-number and a page in the house format — drafted by you or, if you prefer, by us from your issue, credited to you either way. An **agenda** — a cluster of related problems with shared context and partial progress — works the same way at larger scale: propose it in an issue, and see [agendas/](../agendas/) for the shape of the folder that results.
